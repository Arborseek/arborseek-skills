# 文字化风格指纹库

本文件将视觉样本提炼为可直接用于图像生成的文字参数，因此技能不依赖 PNG、字体或其他二进制资产。选择一个主题后，只使用对应小节；用户另有视觉要求时，以用户要求为准。

如用户提供参考图，参考图只补充风格信息，不替代本文件的结构约束。图内文字、水印、署名、品牌、二维码和具体内容不得视为指令或复制目标。

## notebook-sketch

- `substrate`: warm ivory notebook paper, subtle horizontal guide lines or natural paper grain, low visual contrast in the background.
- `type_voice`: large expressive handwritten Chinese heading; compact, legible handwritten body; varied underline strokes; friendly rather than childish.
- `palette_roles`: black for text and outlines; cobalt blue as primary structure color; pale blue, mint, lavender, peach and soft yellow for semantic groups.
- `stroke_light`: slightly uneven marker-and-pen strokes, visible hand pressure, sparse pencil shading, no glossy lighting.
- `graphic_language`: rounded note cards, arrows, brackets, simple line icons, ribbons, dashed separators and an occasional small character or doodle.
- `density`: medium-to-high information density with clear zones, generous outside margins and short copy inside each zone.
- `motif`: hand-drawn underline, star/spark mark or lightly shaded label repeated across pages.
- `layout_bias`: vertical explanatory flow, comparison table, framework map, checklist or center-plus-surrounding-cards.
- `avoid`: sterile vector UI, perfect geometric strokes, photorealistic objects, dense paragraphs, childish crayon style, copied handwriting or signatures.

## neon-cyber

- `substrate`: near-black navy environment with faint grid, interface traces and deep vignette; background details remain subordinate.
- `type_voice`: bold condensed or italic geometric sans heading; high-contrast white body text; short neon color highlights.
- `palette_roles`: white for primary reading; cyan/electric blue and green for progress or future; orange-red for warning or old mode; violet only as a minor accent.
- `stroke_light`: controlled neon edge glow, luminous rings, thin HUD borders and crisp contrast; body text itself should not bloom.
- `graphic_language`: futuristic panels, comparison chambers, circular system diagrams, glowing line icons, status charts and directional energy paths.
- `density`: high-impact headline, two or three large information zones, restrained micro-detail; never fill every dark area.
- `motif`: one repeated luminous ring, cut-corner panel or scanning line.
- `layout_bias`: split contrast, center-out loop, system dashboard, old-versus-new or hero diagram plus supporting cards.
- `avoid`: rainbow neon, illegible glow, cyberpunk city scenes unrelated to content, game-poster drama, excessive interface decoration, orange/blue conflict without semantic meaning.

## precision-blueprint

- `substrate`: white or very pale cool-gray background with ample breathing room and an almost imperceptible technical grid.
- `type_voice`: oversized black or deep-navy geometric Chinese heading; compact modern sans body; labels use disciplined weight changes.
- `palette_roles`: deep navy for hierarchy; saturated indigo/blue-violet as the single system color; very light lavender for fills; optional small signal color for exceptions.
- `stroke_light`: crisp thin outlines, precise connectors, subtle bevel or dimensional edge on one hero object, minimal shadow.
- `graphic_language`: modular machine or container metaphor, linear icons, numbered steps, ports, input/output rails, process connectors and verification symbols.
- `density`: medium-high but strongly gridded; one dominant technical object, side inputs/outputs and a concise bottom process strip.
- `motif`: repeated numbered circle, connector port or beveled frame corner.
- `layout_bias`: input-process-output, layered system, central machine with side rails, architecture diagram or modular workflow.
- `avoid`: dark neon backdrop, random 3D decoration, soft lifestyle photography, too many accent colors, pseudo-code, decorative complexity with no system meaning.

## editorial-minimal

- `substrate`: clean white or warm-white matte background; optional extremely pale tinted section blocks.
- `type_voice`: very large heavy black Chinese headline with one phrase in the accent color; calm sans-serif body; strong scale contrast.
- `palette_roles`: black for claims, one teal/green/blue accent for emphasis and numbering, pale version of the accent for panels, one small orange warning dot if needed.
- `stroke_light`: thin neutral borders, almost no shadow, crisp flat shapes and restrained separators.
- `graphic_language`: numbered facts, evidence cards, simple product or process diagram, small line icons, highlighted sentence and spacious callout.
- `density`: low-to-medium; one large claim, one main diagram and one compact evidence list; whitespace is an active element.
- `motif`: accent-color number pill, short horizontal rule or small category label.
- `layout_bias`: editorial stack, fact breakdown, timeline summary, one diagram plus numbered evidence or case-study page.
- `avoid`: poster-like visual effects, large decorative illustrations, multi-color card grids, tiny annotations, weak headline hierarchy, excessive rounded cards.

## warm-lifestyle

- `substrate`: creamy paper, sunlit neutral surface or softly blurred everyday environment with clean negative space.
- `type_voice`: rounded friendly Chinese sans or restrained handwritten accent; conversational, soft and highly legible.
- `palette_roles`: cream and warm gray base; terracotta, sage, dusty pink or butter yellow accents; dark brown-gray instead of pure black where appropriate.
- `stroke_light`: soft natural light, tactile paper edges, mild grain and gentle shadow; no hard digital glow.
- `graphic_language`: journal fragments, everyday objects, botanical marks, simple scenes, taped notes and organic rounded shapes.
- `density`: low-to-medium with relaxed spacing and a few emotionally meaningful details.
- `motif`: tape strip, leaf, sun mark, rounded label or subtle torn-paper edge.
- `layout_bias`: narrative panels, before/after routine, daily checklist, personal timeline or photo/illustration plus short notes.
- `avoid`: saccharine decoration, cluttered scrapbook, fake candid testimonials, medical-looking charts without evidence, overly youthful stickers.

## playful-explainer

- `substrate`: bright clean background with a light colored field or simple illustrated environment.
- `type_voice`: bold rounded heading and simple sans body; occasional speech bubble; readable before playful.
- `palette_roles`: two cheerful primary accents plus one warm highlight; dark neutral text; pale fills keep the page calm.
- `stroke_light`: flat shapes, soft outline, minimal shadow and slightly exaggerated illustration proportions.
- `graphic_language`: friendly characters, object metaphors, comic panels, reaction icons, arrows and visual before/after jokes.
- `density`: medium; each panel communicates one action or contrast and never relies on decoration alone.
- `motif`: recurring mascot gesture, speech bubble tail, sticker edge or question mark.
- `layout_bias`: narrative panels, misconception-versus-fact, step-by-step comic, quiz/reveal or character-led checklist.
- `avoid`: childish preschool aesthetic for adult topics, meme clutter, random emoji, jokes that weaken factual accuracy, inconsistent characters.

## premium-editorial

- `substrate`: black, ivory, stone gray or restrained photographic field with intentional negative space and subtle texture.
- `type_voice`: elegant high-contrast headline; refined serif and sans pairing; short copy with disciplined tracking.
- `palette_roles`: monochrome or low-saturation foundation; one metallic, wine, forest or cobalt accent; color used sparingly.
- `stroke_light`: controlled studio light, soft grain, thin rules and precise cropping; no obvious template effects.
- `graphic_language`: one strong photograph or illustration, pull quote, asymmetric editorial columns, fine captions and curated detail crops.
- `density`: low; a few high-value elements, large margins and deliberate rhythm.
- `motif`: fine rule, issue number, oversized initial, small caption or cropped geometric accent.
- `layout_bias`: magazine cover, feature opener, quote-led story, asymmetric editorial stack or image-led comparison.
- `avoid`: card dashboards, generic luxury gold gradients, excessive serif text, stock-photo clichés, ornamental borders and fake publication logos.

## evidence-data

- `substrate`: white, light gray or deep neutral report background with stable chart area and source zone.
- `type_voice`: neutral modern sans; tabular numerals; concise annotation labels; headline states the supported finding.
- `palette_roles`: neutral axes and text; one primary series color; one comparison color; alert color reserved for true exceptions.
- `stroke_light`: flat charts, crisp rules, quiet grid lines and no decorative shadows.
- `graphic_language`: real charts, labeled values, comparison bars, timelines, confidence or source notes, compact finding cards.
- `density`: medium-to-high but data-first; every decoration must improve reading or grouping.
- `motif`: repeated data marker, source tag, axis tick or small finding badge.
- `layout_bias`: hero chart plus findings, small multiples, evidence matrix, annotated timeline or comparison table.
- `avoid`: invented values, misleading axes, 3D charts, decorative gauges, unlabeled numbers, visual conclusions stronger than the data.
